System Abstract & Objective

Flowgrid is a specialized infrastructure platform engineered for decentralized electrical distribution networks. It pairs edge microcontroller telemetry nodes with low-latency ingestion servers to give energy operators real-time visibility into transformer loads, line frequency fluctuations, and thermal thresholds.

Operational Role

Founder & Chief Architect: Responsible for complete end-to-end specification—from edge PCB schematics and FreeRTOS firmware to time-series streaming APIs and reactive monitoring consoles.

AquaGuide

aquaguide.in
Open aquaguide.in

System Abstract & Objective

AquaGuide addresses large-scale water distribution inefficiencies through acoustic and ultrasonic sensor arrays. By analyzing micro-vibrations in piping networks, the platform isolates leak locations before surface symptoms appear, while providing facilities and utilities with automated consumption auditing.

Operational Role

Founder & Lead Systems Engineer: Spearheaded the sensor calibration algorithms, battery-efficient telemetry protocols, and the centralized command dashboard for municipal and institutional facilities.

PRIMARY SECTOR Water Resource Intelligence & Infrastructure Monitoring
SENSOR INTERFACES Ultrasonic Time-of-Flight Flow, Hydrostatic Pressure Transducers, LoRaWAN / Cellular IoT
ANALYTICS ENGINE Acoustic Anomaly Detection, Consumption Baseline Forecasting
FRONTEND CLIENT High-Density Asset Map, Automated Incident Dispatcher, React/Node.js
DEPLOYMENT STATUS ● Field Testbeds & Scaling Commercial Rollout
